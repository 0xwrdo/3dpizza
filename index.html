The dough rests for two days. The pizza bakes for ninety seconds.

Dough

Flour, water, salt and a little sourdough starter. It sits in the cold for 48 hours before we stretch it.

Oven

A beechwood fire at around 850°F. A pizza is done in about 90 seconds.

Toppings

San Marzano tomatoes crushed by hand, fior di latte from Agerola, olive oil to finish.
